Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

At BIGX STUDIO, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data, photographs, and event memories you entrust to us.

1. Information Collection

We collect essential information such as names, email addresses, phone/WhatsApp contact information, event dates, itinerary schedules, and venue locations solely to formulate custom proposals and coordinate production crews.

2. Media Security & Archival

All captured raw footage, color-calibrated stills, and audio master recordings are stored securely on private high-speed SSD servers. We never publicly post intimate ritual frames without prior couple approval.

3. Third-Party Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal client information. Data is shared exclusively with our in-house cinematographers and certified Italian leather album binding partners for order fulfillment.

4. Inquiries & Data Rights

For any questions or requests regarding your data, reach out directly at info@bigxstudio.com or via our official WhatsApp concierge.
